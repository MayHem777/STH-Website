# Search engines and AI assistants are welcome (AI search pass 2026-10-27).
# The rule for * already allows everyone; the named AI crawlers below are listed so a
# future blanket block (e.g. a Cloudflare "block AI bots" setting) is easy to spot as a change.
# Search/answer bots (ChatGPT search, Claude, Perplexity) are what put us in AI answers;
# training bots (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot) teach the
# models that Sunshine Tiny Houses exists. To opt out of training only, change their Allow to Disallow.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /admin/

User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
User-agent: ChatGPT-User
User-agent: GPTBot
# Anthropic: Claude search, Claude fetching on a user's request, model training
User-agent: Claude-SearchBot

User-agent: ClaudeBot
# Perplexity

User-agent: Perplexity-User

User-agent: Google-Extended
