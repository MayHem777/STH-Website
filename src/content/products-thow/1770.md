## The 1770

Named after the historic Queensland town, the 1770 is Sunshine Tiny Houses' most compact model — and one of the most popular. At 6 metres long, it proves you don't need to compromise on quality or comfort to live small.

Unlike most tiny houses, the 1770 features a ground-floor bedroom — no climbing a loft ladder at the end of the day. This makes it ideal as a short-stay rental, a primary residence for solo occupants, or a retreat on your property.

The 1770 is the only design in our tiny house range with no loft. Everything is on one level inside a 6 × 2.5 m footprint, including the sleeping space for two, so there's no ladder or stair to deal with at the end of the day or for short-stay guests.

It can be built on a dual axle trailer rated to 3.5 tonnes ATM, or a triple axle trailer rated to 4.5 tonnes, each with a VIN plate, four jack legs and electric brakes with a breakaway system. Inside, it has the same full-size oven and 2-burner gas cooktop as our larger models, and its shower base is 1200 × 900 mm, bigger than the 900 × 900 mm base listed on most of our loft models.

The standard exterior is Weathertex Weathergroove Woodsman painted in Monument, with Pisces vinyl plank floors from the Clever Choice range. Like all our tiny houses on wheels, the 1770 is classed as a caravan rather than a building, so the rules that apply are your local council's rules for caravan living.

### Inclusions

Every 1770 comes with a custom-built trailer (3.5T or 4.5T ATM options), Bluescope steel frame, Colorbond roof with R2.5 insulation, Weathertex exterior cladding, full kitchen and bathroom fit-out, split-system air conditioning, and instantaneous gas hot water.
