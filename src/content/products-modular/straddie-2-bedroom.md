## Straddie — 2 Bedroom

While exuding an air of cosy charm, this granny flat is also designed with practicality. Clever storage solutions maximise space efficiency, ensuring every item has its place without compromising style. Whether you're a minimalist at heart or a collector of cherished mementos, there's ample room to organise your belongings and create a clutter-free environment.

The 2-bedroom Straddie takes the one-bedroom layout and adds a second bedroom, stretching the home to 13.5 × 3.8 m. The two bedrooms sit at opposite ends, with the kitchen, living room and wet areas between them, so neither bedroom shares a wall with the other. The main bedroom is drawn with a queen bed and a sliding-door wardrobe; the second fits two single beds and has a built-in wardrobe of its own, which suits children, guests or a home office.

In the middle, the kitchen has an integrated dining island with stool seating, facing the living room and the sliding entry door. Past the living room are the shared vanity and laundry, with the bathroom (toilet and an 850 × 1850 mm shower) behind them. Raked ceilings, skylights and large windows keep the long plan bright.

Next to the Daintree, which is 0.5 m longer, the Straddie gives the second bedroom two single beds and a proper wardrobe, where the Daintree's second room is set up as an office with a desk. It suits a full-time home, a holiday retreat or an investment.
