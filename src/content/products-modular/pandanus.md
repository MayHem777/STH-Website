## Pandanus

Step inside to discover a simple yet chic space that radiates warmth and cosiness. The open-plan layout seamlessly combines the living, dining, and kitchen areas, creating an intimate ambience perfect for relaxation and unwinding.

Step outside to discover your own private outdoor oasis, where a secluded patio beckons you to unwind amidst the beauty of nature.

The Pandanus shares its 10.5 × 3.8 m footprint with the one-bedroom Straddie but organises it differently. The laundry is not a room: the washing machine, laundry, pantry and storage all sit behind a row of full-height cupboards along the back wall, which frees the middle of the home for a larger open-plan kitchen and living area.

The plan is drawn with the optional 3.0 × 3.8 m deck (about 12 m²) at the living end. Glass doors open from the deck straight into the lounge, and a second entry door on the long side comes in by the kitchen. The kitchen is built around an island with the sink and stools along one side, with the bathroom behind it: toilet, vanity and a 900 × 1850 mm shower. The bedroom closes off the far end with a queen bed and a sliding-door wardrobe.

Choose the Pandanus if you want indoor-outdoor living and storage you can close the doors on. Choose the Straddie if you'd rather have a separate laundry room with its own basin.
