Common Questions

Answered Before You Have to Ask

The questions we hear most from Queensland homeowners — straight answers.

What can I build on my property?

A granny flat, secondary dwelling, family accommodation unit, or modular home — depending on your block size, zoning, and council requirements. The quickest way to know is to request a personalised quote — we'll review your block and give you a straight answer.

Do you manage council approval?

Yes. We manage the full approval process — development applications, secondary dwelling permits, engineering reports, and certifier engagement. Your only role is to sign where we ask you to. We handle everything else.

What does "turn-key" mean in practice?

Flooring, joinery, kitchen, bathroom, appliances (where specified), and utility connections. You receive keys to a dwelling someone can move into on the same day. Everything is specified clearly in your quote before you sign anything.

How long does the whole process take?

Approval timelines differ across Queensland councils, and project scope affects build duration. We'll give you a realistic timeline specific to your project during your initial assessment call — no generic estimates.

How much does a secondary dwelling cost?

Studios start from $110k, one-bedroom homes from $140k, and two-bedroom homes from $195k — all incl. GST, ex-factory. Delivery, site works and council approvals are quoted separately for your block. The full brochure with every model's price is available above, no form required, and a personalised quote is free with no obligation.

Can I rent it out straight after handover?

Yes — once the certificate of occupancy is issued, you can lease it immediately. We'll advise on the documentation required for a compliant tenancy arrangement as part of the handover process.

What areas do you service?

We deliver within 1,250km of our Noosaville factory — South East Queensland, Northern NSW, Wide Bay, Central Queensland and as far north as Townsville. That comfortably covers Gympie, Bundaberg, Hervey Bay, Gladstone, Rockhampton and Mackay. Further afield? Get in touch and we'll let you know what's possible.

What happens after I submit the form?

Mitch or Nat will personally review your details and reply by email with model recommendations and pricing to suit what you're after — usually within a couple of business days. It's a straightforward, personal reply — no call centre, no sales pitch.
