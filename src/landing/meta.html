How We Build

Built in a Factory. Delivered Complete.

Unlike a traditional on-site build, a Sunshine Tiny Houses modular home is constructed almost entirely inside our Noosaville workshop — then delivered to your block ready to install.

🏭

Built Under Controlled Conditions

Your home takes shape inside our factory — no weather delays, no warping timber, no waiting on tradies between rain days. Quality doesn't depend on the forecast.

🚚

Delivered & Crane-Lifted Onto Your Block

On install day, your modular home arrives on the truck and is crane-lifted into position. What would take months on-site is done in days.

🚷

No Months of On-Site Disruption

Because 95% of the build happens off your property, you don't have tradies camped at your house for weeks. Your block stays yours until delivery day.

✅

Certified and Move-In Ready

It arrives with the bulk of construction already complete. On-site work — stairs, decks, and service connections — is handled quickly, followed by a final inspection. Far less disruption than a traditional build.
